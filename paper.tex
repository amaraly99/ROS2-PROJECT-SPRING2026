\subsection{SLAM Algorithm Selection and Pareto Ablation}
\label{sec:slam_pareto}
We report the median over 10 runs per sequence because some SLAM algorithms,
notably OV$^{2}$SLAM and ORB-SLAM3, can exhibit inflated means from a single
bad run. All values are APE RMSE; the literature frequently refers to this
metric as ATE.
Rows highlighted in \colorbox{measuredgreen}{green} are based on confirmed,
fully-measured benchmark batches; all other rows show representative values
pending complete statistical sweeps.
