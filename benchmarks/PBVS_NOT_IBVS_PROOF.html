Proof A: the algebra

With the pose as feature, the closed loop is a straight exponential on each axis, independent of the camera.
Point motion. The camera moves with velocity v (right, down, forward) and no rotation. The target point P = (Xc, Yc, Zc) in the camera frame moves as dP/dt = −v. So the interaction matrix is L = −I3. It has no dependence on Z, f or the image.
Control law. Choose v = λ (P − P*), with P* = (0, 0, Zc*). In the body frame: vx = λ(Zc − Zc*), vy = −λ Xc, vz = −λ Yc (body: vy left+, vz up+).
Closed loop. Let e = P − P*. Then de/dt = −λ e, so e(t) = e(0) · exp(−λ t). The time constant is 1/λ = 3.33 s for λ = 0.3. It does not contain Zc, fx, fy or known_h.
Straight line. P(t) = P* + (P(0) − P*) e−λt. All points lie on the line from P(0) to P*. Equal λ on the three axes is the condition. Euler steps keep this exactly: each step scales e by (1 − λ dt).
Stability with limits. V = ½|e|². Each clamp keeps the sign of its command equal to the sign of its error component. So dV/dt ≤ 0, and dV/dt = 0 only at e = 0 (or on the one-sided vx floor, see below).
Contrast. Today's vertical law is vz = −0.5 · fy·Yc/(240·Zc). Then dYc/dt = −(0.5 fy / 240) · Yc / Zc. The time constant is 0.866 · Zc. It grows with range. That is the image-feature signature.
