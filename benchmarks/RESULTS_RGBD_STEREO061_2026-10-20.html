RGB-D and stereo 0.61 m results

Unverified, Amar pushed on date 2026-10-20 but he will check it and update the readme.md

This report collects the RGB-D results (campaign versoH, 2026-10-19/20) and the stereo results with the 0.61 m baseline (2026-10-18). All runs fly the same scripted path. The RGB-D and stereo sets used different run protocols; section 5 lists the differences.

1 · Summary

Main results

  • No camera mode reaches the 0.10 m target yet. The best healthy-run means are OV²SLAM-A stereo 0.075 m (8 runs, not 10), ORB-SLAM3 stereo 0.108 m (7 runs) and ORB-SLAM2 RGB-D 0.119 m (10 runs).
  • RGB-D, ORB family: ORB-SLAM2 0.119 ± 0.014 m and ORB-SLAM3 0.125 ± 0.021 m, 10 of 10 attempts healthy each, no blow-ups. The VERSO reference for ORB-SLAM2 RGB-D is 0.108 m.
  • RGB-D, RTAB-Map: 0.235 ± 0.087 m, and only 14 of 32 attempts were healthy.
  • Stereo: every arm except RTAB-Map had blow-ups (ATE > 1 m) in some runs. The stereo sets were not repeated to 10 healthy runs.
  • Two fixes made these numbers possible: OpenGV built into OV²SLAM (stereo had run away to 10⁶ m without it), and the ORB RGB-D virtual baseline set to 0.61 m (was 0.11 m: ATE 0.41 m).
2 · Shared setup

Flight, camera and scorer

ItemValue
Flightmatlab/slam_traj_probe_unified.m (stereo) and matlab/slam_traj_probe_rgbd_unified.m (RGB-D) differ by one comment line only, so both modes fly the same commanded path. Mode TRAJ_MODE = "sixdof", four phases:
  1. Wait: the drone hovers at the start (15.12, 3.84, 7.5), heading 357.4° (pointing at the sign), while SLAM starts and the runner opens the record gate.
  2. Side step (start-up leg): 3.0 m sideways at vy −0.8 m/s with a small climb at vz +0.2 m/s (INIT_LATERAL, INIT_VY, INIT_VZ), then 2 s still (INIT_SETTLE); limit 20 s. Sideways motion gives SLAM parallax to start its map; forward motion toward the scene gives almost none. Measured: y 3.84 → 0.76 m, z 7.5 → 8.27 m.
  3. Wavy cruise: forward at 2.0 m/s for 13 m (1 wave of 13 m; run values vx 2.0, cycles 1, wavelength 13). The path is a helix: sideways ±0.5 m/s and up-down ±0.35 m/s, 90° apart, plus a drift that brings the drone to the sign's side and height. The cruise is gated on true forward distance, so a slower simulation changes the duration, not the path. The camera sweeps in yaw and pitch: commanded ±8° yaw (SIX_YAW_DEG) and ±3° pitch (SIX_PITCH_DEG); measured ±7.1° yaw (352.9°–367.1°) and −2.6° to +2.3° pitch, because the angle controllers lag the command.
  4. Approach: a P controller (gain 0.8, speed limit 1.2 m/s, plus a sideways wiggle of 0.5 m/s at 1 rad/s) flies to the target point 3 m in front of the sign (STANDOFF = 3.0) with heading 0°. It stops when x and z are within 0.15 m, or after 25 s. It does not correct y: the stereo OpenGV run ended at (31.96, 3.44, 3.08), 0.52 m to the side of the target point.
Measured against the locked gates: camera within −4.5° to +9.7° of the bearing to the sign (G4 limit 15°), yaw rate at most about 3.4°/s (G6 limit 15°/s).
Run flagsEvery run: ./run_slam_benchmark.sh <slam> <tag> --runs N --camera-yaw-offset-deg 0 --yaw-amp-deg 0 --init-axis lateral plus the camera flags.
  • --camera-yaw-offset-deg 0: no fixed camera turn. MATLAB (hil_session_start_slam.m) runs the unified probe; a non-zero offset would switch to the "wide view" script slam_traj_probe_wide.m, which turns the camera away from the sign.
  • --yaw-amp-deg 0: sets YAW_AMP_DEG, which only the "gentle" mode uses. In sixdof mode the yaw sweep comes from SIX_YAW_DEG = 8.0, so this flag does not switch the sweep off. Describe the runs as "slow ±7° yaw sweep during the cruise", not "fixed yaw".
  • --init-axis lateral: the start-up leg goes sideways (phase 2). The other value starts along the path and also switches MATLAB to the wide-view script.
  • --camera-mode stereo --baseline 0.61: MATLAB loads hil_closed_loop_baseline_061 (right camera at −0.61 m), the Pi starts the stereo stack, and each SLAM reads its 0.61 m stereo calibration.
  • --camera-mode rgbd: MATLAB loads hil_closed_loop_baseline_RGBD (one camera, z-depth 16UC1 mm, same stamp as colour), and the Pi starts the RGB-D stack. ORB-SLAM2 and ORB-SLAM3 turn each depth value into a virtual stereo match with a 0.61 m virtual baseline (Camera.bf 337.94 / Stereo.b 0.61); RTAB-Map uses the depth directly.
Camerafx 554, 640×480, mount (0.2, 0, 0.2) m, 5° down. Stereo: right camera at −0.61 m (models hil_closed_loop_baseline_061). RGB-D: one camera with z-depth, 16UC1 mm (model hil_closed_loop_baseline_RGBD).
Depth checkz-depth, within about 1 % of the image geometry (1764 stereo points; stop sign 20.35 m against 20.13 m expected), aligned to the pixel, same stamp as colour.
Scorerharness scorer: Sim(3) fit (Umeyama) on the scored window; the same for every arm.

7 · Open issues

What is still unresolved

  • No mode reaches ≤ 0.10 m with 10 healthy runs.
  • "No GT motion" failures: the drone did not fly (5 RTAB-Map RGB-D attempts, 3 ORB-SLAM2 stereo attempts). This is a harness fault, not a SLAM fault. Cause not found.
  • evo_ape "degenerate covariance" failures (9 RTAB-Map RGB-D attempts, 1 OV²SLAM stereo attempt): the scorer cannot fit the trajectory. Cause not found.
  • Stereo blow-ups (3–6 m) in OV²SLAM-A, ORB-SLAM2 and ORB-SLAM3. Cause not found.
  • d_target_m ("end distance to sign") measures to the old sign (35.5, 23.7): known bug, not reported.
  • The OpenGV build lives only on the Pi (clone + Docker build); it is not reproducible from the repository yet.
  • The locked gate script benchmarks/acceptance_gates.py reads only reports/slam_final_*, so it does not see the versoH reports.
